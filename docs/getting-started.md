# Getting started

Mixdown has a small main window and also lives in your Mac's menu bar. The window has a sidebar
with a search field and the **Record** button (●) at the top, and your recordings below it under
**Today**, **Previous 7 Days** and then each month. The cog above the sidebar, next to the sidebar
button, opens Settings.

## Setup assistant

The first time Mixdown opens, the setup assistant walks you through:

1. **Choose a Notes Folder**: where your notes go, for example a new `Mixdown` folder in
   Documents (use **New Folder** in the dialog). See [Notes](notes.md#the-notes-folder).
2. **Pick a Speech Model** and download it. See [Speech models](speech-models.md).
3. **What to Record**: your microphone, system audio or both, and allowing the microphone.
4. **Calls and Notifications**: call detection and allowing notifications. See
   [Recording calls](calls.md#call-detection).
5. **Always at Hand**: the global shortcut and launching at login.

**Help → Setup Assistant…** opens it again at any time.

If you skip it, open **Record** and click **Choose Folder…** and **Download Model** there.
Recording stays off until both are done. Record shows what's missing.

## Your first recording

1. Open **Record** (⌘1). It shows the big record button and what the next recording uses:
   microphone, system audio, speech model, language and notes folder.
2. Pick your inputs in the **Recording** menu (a microphone, **System Audio**, or both), or with
   **Change…** next to the setup summary on Record.
3. Click the red button, or press ⌘R, to start recording. From any other app, press ⌃⌥⌘R.
4. Click it again, or press ⌘R, to stop.

Soon after you stop, the note appears under **Today** in the sidebar and opens. A **Note Ready**
notification says so too, so you can stop from the menu bar and carry on elsewhere.

## Make it yours

- **Settings → General → Launch Mixdown at login** keeps Mixdown in the menu bar.
- **Settings → Design → Appearance** picks Auto, Light or Dark.
- **Settings → Transcription** picks the speech model, the language and the names of the
  speakers.

See [Settings](settings.md) for everything else.
